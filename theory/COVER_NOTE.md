# Cover Note: How to Read This Paper Defensively

**Document:** Global Flood Hydrotectonics: A Quantitative Theory of Rapid Continental Reorganization
**Version:** 2.5
**Author:** James (JD) Longmire

---

## Purpose of This Note

This cover note explains how to evaluate the accompanying preprint on its own terms, rather than on assumptions imported from other frameworks. It is intended for reviewers, critics, and engaged readers.

---

## What This Paper Is

1. **A working research programme**, not a finished theory
2. **A test of physical plausibility**, not a claim of empirical confirmation
3. **An explicit constraint analysis**, not a defense of all flood geology claims
4. **A Lakatosian structure** with identified hard core, protective belt, and falsifiers

---

## What This Paper Is Not

1. **Not a claim that the Flood is proven** - it explores whether a specific mechanism is physically coherent
2. **Not an argument from authority** - all quantitative claims trace to calculations
3. **Not a complete explanation** - it explicitly lists what it does and does not claim
4. **Not immune to falsification** - falsifiers are identified and would be accepted if triggered

---

## How to Evaluate the Paper Fairly

### Do Engage With:
- The energy budget calculations (Section 4)
- The heat flux comparison (hydrotectonic vs. conventional)
- The mineralogical evidence for prior water mobility
- The explicit falsifiers and open questions
- The distinction between core claims and auxiliary hypotheses

### Do Not:
- Dismiss based on the conclusion rather than the physics
- Assume claims that are not made (check "What the Model Does Not Claim")
- Attack auxiliary hypotheses as if they were the core
- Demand completeness that the paper explicitly disclaims

---

## The Central Question

> "Can a large, connected detachment network plausibly reach near-lithostatic pore pressures and sustain thin-film lubrication over the required scales without choking itself off through drainage, flashing, cavitation, or mineral sealing?"

The paper argues: preliminary analysis shows a non-empty parameter regime where this works. Constitutive laws for permeability evolution remain unvalidated. That is the hinge.

## Intellectual Honesty Markers

The paper explicitly:
- Distinguishes STIPULATED, DERIVED, and ASSUMED parameters
- Separates novel predictions from reinterpretations
- Identifies the highest-risk cross-cutting constraint (timescale)
- Ranks issues by vulnerability to critique
- States what would falsify the model
- Marks speculative claims (e.g., geomagnetic excursions) as such

---

## Request to Reviewers

If you find errors in the physics or calculations, please identify them specifically. The author welcomes correction.

If you disagree with the framework's assumptions, please state which assumptions you reject and why. Philosophical disagreement is valid but should be distinguished from physical error.
