# Water Budget Symmetry Argument

**Date:** 2025-12-18
**Status:** Defense Material

---

## Core Observation

There is **no fully integrated, quantitative model** that closes the pathway by which Earth's interior water was sourced, transported, incorporated into mantle minerals, and retained at scale.

What exists instead is a **patchwork of mechanisms**, each locally plausible, none globally closed.

This matters for evaluating water-budget objections against alternative models.

---

## What Mainstream Geology Has (Partial, Fragmented)

### 1. Source of Water

- **Assumed:** Surface ocean water
- **Mechanism:** Hydration of oceanic crust and upper mantle at mid-ocean ridges and bending faults
- **Status:** Well supported locally, not globally quantified

### 2. Transport Mechanism

- **Assumed:** Long-term subduction of hydrated slabs
- **Evidence:** Seismic imaging of slabs to 410-660 km
- **Status:** Observed transport, but:
  - Slab coherence is variable
  - Dehydration occurs at multiple depths
  - Transport efficiency is poorly constrained

### 3. Entrapment Mechanism

- **Assumed:** Incorporation into wadsleyite and ringwoodite during phase transitions
- **Evidence:** Experimental mineral physics
- **Status:** Strong laboratory support, weak global mass-balance closure

### 4. Retention Over Time

- **Assumed:** Slow diffusion, metastable storage
- **Status:** Poorly constrained, heavily model-dependent

---

## What Does NOT Exist

A single integrated model that says:

> "Here is how X ocean-masses of water move from the surface into the mantle, how long it takes, how efficiently it happens, and why it stops where it does."

That model does not exist.

---

## Where the Mainstream Hand-Waves

The deep-time story implicitly relies on:

- Billions of years as a solvent for missing closure
- Spatial averaging to smooth inefficiencies
- Reinterpretation when mass balance doesn't quite close

This is how big geophysical problems are often handled. But it means critics **cannot claim** this process is fully understood or closed.

When someone says "There isn't enough water for a Flood," they are standing on a model that **also does not close the water cycle** end-to-end.

---

## The Symmetry Argument

We are NOT arguing:
- "My model is complete and theirs isn't."

We ARE arguing:
- "Both models rely on incomplete water-cycle closure, so water-budget objections cannot be used as impossibility claims."

**Our model:** rapid mobilization → storage → sequestration

**Mainstream model:** slow mobilization → transport → sequestration

Neither has a fully closed, globally quantified entrapment pathway.

---

## The Asymmetry to Press

Deep time assumes:
- Gradual hydration
- Slab transport
- Mineral incorporation
- Retention

But **cannot specify**:
- Total water mass transferred
- Efficiency
- Timescale consistency
- Why the transition zone holds *that* much and not more

Yet it treats this as settled enough to dismiss alternatives.

**That's the pressure point.**

---

## Deployable Statement

> There is no fully integrated, quantitative model that closes the pathway by which Earth's interior water was sourced, transported, incorporated into mantle minerals, and retained at scale. What exists are well-supported local mechanisms stitched together across deep time. That may be sufficient for a working framework, but it means water-budget objections cannot be used as proofs of impossibility against alternative models that propose different mobilization histories.

This is accurate and restrained.

---

## Discipline Points

**DO NOT say:**
- "They don't know how the water got there."

They do, in pieces.

**DO say:**
- "The process is not globally closed or quantitatively bounded."

This is true and defensible.
