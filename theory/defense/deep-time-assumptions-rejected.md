# Deep-Time Assumptions Rejected

**Date:** 2025-12-18
**Status:** Defense Material - Framework Challenge

---

## Preamble

I am not disputing that geology contains real data. I am disputing the **interpretive assumptions** that convert that data into a multi-hundred-million-year narrative. My model challenges those assumptions directly.

---

## The Seven Assumptions

### 1. That most stratified rock records slow, low-energy deposition by default

This assumption is foundational to deep time. It treats high-energy deposition as exceptional and local. I reject this. Large-scale, water-dominated systems naturally produce laterally extensive, ordered strata rapidly. Order does not imply duration. It implies sequence.

### 2. That stratigraphic order uniquely implies elapsed time

Deep time equates vertical order with temporal separation. Catastrophic systems also produce order through hydraulic sorting, ecological zonation, mobility, and burial sequence. The existence of order is not diagnostic of timescale.

### 3. That basin geometry is inherited slowly and only modified by later events

Deep time presumes that major basins formed gradually and that impacts or catastrophes merely overprint them. My model treats basin geometry itself as an outcome of catastrophic restructuring. If the basin is a product, not a premise, the inferred chronology collapses.

### 4. That radiometric systems remained closed and monotonic through deep history

Radiometric ages depend on assumptions about initial conditions, long-term closure, and undisturbed decay histories. A global hydraulic catastrophe involving extreme pressure, heat, fluid flow, and rapid mineral recrystallization directly violates those assumptions. That does not make radiometric data meaningless, but it does make deep-time interpretations model-dependent rather than assumption-free.

### 5. That time is the primary explanatory variable

Deep time repeatedly uses "given enough time" to solve heat, transport, sorting, preservation, migration, and water-cycle problems. My model replaces time as the solvent with pressure, connectivity, and catastrophic energy input. This is not an appeal to miracles. It is a change of causal regime.

### 6. That present-day Earth is a reliable template for past system architecture

Deep time implicitly projects today's compartmentalized crust, sealed basins, and limited permeability backward. My model proposes a fundamentally different pre-Flood architecture with global hydraulic connectivity. If initial conditions differ, extrapolation fails.

### 7. That alternative regimes must meet a higher standard of completion to be admissible

Deep-time geology tolerates unresolved hinges by distributing risk across time and auxiliary assumptions. My model is criticized for incompleteness precisely where deep time is allowed interpretive flexibility. I reject that asymmetry.

---

## Summary

I am not claiming my model is finished. I am claiming that deep time is not assumption-neutral, and that its assumptions are precisely what my model is designed to test.

The debate is not data versus faith. It is **which causal regime better accounts for the data without insulating itself from failure**.
