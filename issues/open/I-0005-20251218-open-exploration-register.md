# Issue: Open Exploration Register

**Status:** Open
**Priority:** High
**Category:** Research Programme / Constraints
**Phase:** All

---

## Description

Comprehensive register of open issues and opportunities for further exploration. Each item is phrased as a question under constraint, not a claim, so it survives scrutiny.

> The following issues are identified not as unresolved flaws but as active constraints that define the empirical risk and future testability of the hydrotectonic research programme.

---

## 1. Timescale Compression

**Question:** Can the proposed hydrotectonic, hydration, and depositional processes proceed at flood-year timescales without violating known chemical, mechanical, or magnetic relaxation limits?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Hydration reaction kinetics under extreme pressure and temperature
- Paleomagnetic remanence acquisition rates in rapidly deposited sediments
- Core-mantle response times for geomagnetic excursions

**Why it matters:** This is the most common secular objection. If timescales fail, multiple downstream claims fail.

---

## 2. Permeability Evolution and Collapse

**Question:** Can global-scale permeability increase rapidly during hydrotectonic failure and then collapse fast enough to trap fluids post-event?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Channeled-porosity vs compartmentalized flow
- Reaction-driven sealing mechanisms
- Evidence for abrupt permeability loss in the rock record

**Why it matters:** This controls both water mobilization and post-Flood sequestration.

---

## 3. Energy Partitioning Robustness

**Question:** Is the dominance of hydrotectonic work over geothermal and magmatic contributions stable across reasonable parameter bounds?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Sensitivity of heat flux to pore pressure assumptions
- Upper bounds on flood-year geothermal output
- Dissipation pathways between mechanical, thermal, and chemical sinks

**Why it matters:** This underwrites the heat problem resolution.

---

## 4. Water Budget Quantification

**Question:** Is the inferred volume of once-mobile water consistent with known mineral hydration capacities and plausible mobilization efficiencies?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Fraction of mantle and lithosphere realistically hydrated during the event
- Flow-rate constraints for deep water release
- Resequestration completeness post-event

**Why it matters:** This directly engages the "not enough water" critique.

---

## 5. Mineralogical Discrimination

**Question:** Do hydration textures, distributions, or isotopic signatures distinguish rapid global hydration from prolonged subduction-driven hydration?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Abrupt vs gradational hydration fronts
- Overprinting signatures
- Depth distribution mismatches with modern subduction geometry

**Why it matters:** This is a potential empirical discriminator rather than a philosophical debate.

---

## 6. Sediment Transport and Sorting

**Question:** Can hydrotectonic-driven turbidity and hypercanic processes reproduce observed sediment volumes and sorting patterns without invoking long timescales?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Grain-size distributions
- Basin-margin megabreccias
- Ecological vs hydraulic sorting signatures

**Why it matters:** This connects deep mechanics to surface geology.

---

## 7. Geomagnetic Excursions

**Question:** Can rapid mass redistribution, fluid flow, and thermal boundary perturbations plausibly generate multiple geomagnetic excursions within a compressed interval?

**Key sub-issues:**
- Distinction between excursions and full reversals
- Core-mantle coupling thresholds
- Signal preservation vs depositional overprinting

**Why it matters:** This is derivative, not core, but potentially powerful if supported.

---

## 8. Falsifiability Targets

**Question:** Which single observation or measurement would most decisively falsify the hydrotectonic framework?

**Candidate targets:**
- Absence of rapid-hydration mineral textures
- Incompatible heat flux bounds
- Paleomagnetic evidence requiring long transitional intervals

**Why it matters:** Explicit falsifiers strengthen credibility even if they are never triggered.
