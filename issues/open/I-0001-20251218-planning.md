# Issue: Credibility Roadmap - From Plausible to Fully Defensible

**Status:** Open
**Priority:** High
**Category:** Physics / Documentation

---

## Description

A systematic plan to take the hydrotectonic Flood mechanism from "plausible" to "fully credible" in the only sense that matters: **it survives constraint tests with explicit margins**, and its failure modes are quantified.

---

## Current Gaps That Block "Full Credibility"

| Gap | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Permeability evolution closure** | No defensible constitutive bounds for k(γ̇,σ',D) and φ(γ̇,σ',D) under extreme shear at relevant scales |
| **Coupled stability window** | No regime map showing a non-empty parameter region where pressure support persists without runaway drainage, cavitation, phase change, or sink localization |
| **Supply constraints** | Q_supply and storage assumptions need derivations and upper bounds; currently stipulated |
| **Drainage and sink geometry** | Network vs sink-dominated topology is asserted conceptually, not demonstrated |
| **Thermal feedback** | Thermal pressurization, phase boundaries, and rupture/cavitation thresholds not bounded against energy budget |
| **Link to observables** | Weak mapping from model outputs to testable field signatures |

---

## Phase 0: Repo Discipline and Claim-Scope Locking

**Goal:** Prevent goalpost-moving accusations

**Deliverables:**
- A "Model Contract" README: what is claimed, what is not claimed, what would falsify it
- Versioned assumptions table: k, φ, β_t, L, μ, ΔP, σ_n, v, supply sources, geometry
- One-page falsifier list: explicit inequalities and observational disconfirmers

**Success Criterion:**
A skeptic can't say you're moving goalposts without being dishonest.

---

## Phase 1: Minimal Coupled Model (The Actual Hinge)

**Goal:** Replace "diffusion estimate" with the smallest system that includes the failure modes

**Deliverables:**
- 1D and 2D PDE model: Darcy + storage + sources + evolving permeability
- Two permeability laws, bracketed:
  - **Dilation-damage dominated:** k increases with shear/damage
  - **Compaction-sealing dominated:** k decreases with effective stress and strain
- Two geometries:
  - **Distributed network** (many conduits, no single sink)
  - **Sink-dominated** (localized drains)

**Success Criterion:**
Produce a stability window plot: regions where σ' = σ_n - P stays below a threshold for a required slip duration.

---

## Phase 2: Dimensionless Regime Map

**Goal:** Make arguments portable and non-handwavy

**Deliverables:**
Define 4-6 dimensionless numbers:
- R₁ = t_shear / t_hyd (slip vs hydraulic response)
- R₂ = Q_supply / Q_drain (support vs loss)
- R₃ = t_thermal / t_shear (heating vs motion)
- R₄ = ΔP / σ_n (fraction of lithostatic support)

Regime diagrams showing when the system:
- sustains overpressure
- valves episodically
- collapses into drains

**Success Criterion:**
The hinge is no longer "story + numbers," it's "regime membership + margins."

---

## Phase 3: Supply and Storage Bounds

**Goal:** Remove "stipulated supply" as a criticism

**Deliverables:**
Conservative lower and upper bounds on storage and release:
- Crustal aquifers and pore volume
- Metamorphic/dehydration contributions
- Mantle wedge/serpentinization-type analog bounds (use cautiously)
- Surface injection plausibility where applicable

A supply sensitivity sweep: show how stability window shrinks as Q_supply drops by 10×, 100×.

**Success Criterion:**
Critics must argue against stated bounds, not accuse you of inventing numbers.

---

## Phase 4: Thermal Pressurization and Phase Stability

**Goal:** Close the "flash steam / cavitation / runaway" objections with bounded criteria

**Deliverables:**
- Add thermal pressurization coupling: shear heating → T → P(T)
- Cavitation and phase boundary checks:
  - Conditions under which P drops below vapor pressure locally
  - Conditions under which T crosses boiling at confining pressure
- Show whether thermal pressurization is stabilizing or destabilizing in your regime map

**Success Criterion:**
A clear statement: "For these parameter ranges, phase change is avoided or confined; beyond them the model fails."

---

## Phase 5: Scaling from Block-Slip to Continent-Scale Transport

**Goal:** Remove the "toy model" dismissal

**Deliverables:**
Hierarchical scaling argument:
- **Micro:** shear zone thickness, damage spacing
- **Meso:** block size distributions and conduit spacing
- **Macro:** required displacement and average effective friction

Demonstrate energy closure using improved friction law and pressure field results.

**Success Criterion:**
A skeptic can no longer say "your mechanism can't scale" without attacking the scaling assumptions explicitly.

---

## Phase 6: Observable Predictions and Discriminators

**Goal:** Make the programme testable against data, not just debated

**Deliverables:**
5-10 predicted signatures that differ by regime:
- Spatial statistics of laterally extensive event beds vs sink-dominated drainage features
- Expected distribution of fluid-escape structures tied to conduit spacing
- Predicted patterns of rapid-burial taphonomy correlated with specific depositional packages

One "next decade" discriminator: a single empirical test that would strongly favor one programme.

**Success Criterion:**
The programme can be evaluated without arguing metaphysics first.

---

## Phase 7: External Validation Track

**Goal:** Convert "GitHub project" into recognized research output

**Deliverables:**
- Preprint "Stability Window for Hydrotectonic Seepage Support" with regime maps and falsifiers
- Reproducible code release with tests and parameter sets
- Invite critique from domain specialists; log issues publicly

**Success Criterion:**
Independent criticism becomes structured inputs to the model, not dismissive noise.

---

## Definition of "Fully Credible"

At completion, the model will have:

1. A non-empty stability window with margins
2. Conservative supply and storage bounds that keep the system in that window
3. Thermal and cavitation thresholds shown not automatically crossed
4. Scaling from local slip to macro transport with explicit assumptions
5. Discriminating predictions, not just explanations
